# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and constructive
environment for everyone participating in the Nano-Stream project.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully
- provide constructive technical feedback
- focus criticism on ideas and implementations rather than individuals
- respect different backgrounds and levels of experience
- act professionally in issues, discussions, and pull requests

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment or discrimination
- personal attacks
- deliberate intimidation
- publishing private information without permission
- abusive or threatening communication
- disruptive behavior that prevents constructive technical discussion

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and discussions
that violate this Code of Conduct.

Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent restriction
of participation.

## Reporting

Concerns about unacceptable behavior should be reported privately to the
repository maintainer through the contact information associated with the
GitHub profile.
